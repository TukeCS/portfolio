trwm live demo

A browser mock-up of trwm. Click the screen and use the keys. Edit the config on the right and it reloads as you type, like the real one.

    Keys

    In trwm every key is held with Mod (Super). In the browser the OS usually grabs Super, so here you can press the keys on their own once the screen is focused. Alt works as Mod too.
